# Three-minute demonstration and pitch guardrails

Derived from Master Book chapters 20–22. No completed product, evidence results or customer traction is assumed at kit publication.

## Timeline
| Time | Stage moment | Evidence to show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Contractor claims a fictional maintenance job is complete; INR 5,000 mock invoice | Buyer pain |
| 0:25–0:50 | Issue challenge and show denied L0 action | No automatic AI authority |
| 0:50–1:20 | Submit incomplete/contradictory image; view model observations, reasons and restricted tier | Fail-closed evidence path |
| 1:20–1:50 | Submit valid consented demo image; reach L2; separate manager signs off mock completion | Distinct responsibilities |
| 1:50–2:20 | Execute independent labeled policy tests; real weak-policy failure and engineer alert | Controlled iterative assurance |
| 2:20–2:45 | Explain target buyer, initial value hypothesis and integration pricing option | Business viability |
| 2:45–3:00 | Gemini now, future Gemma option, model-independent policy, tagline | Architecture and differentiation |

## Never claim without actual validation
- Frauds eliminated; real repairs certified; immutable audit; complete prompt injection protection; no competition; secure in all industries.
- Zero false approvals or quantified improvement without exact sample size, source, evaluated policy/model versions and both error types.
- Feature deployed or live Gemini used without observed successful test. In mock fallback say `Synthetic fixture` / `Recorded prior real run` explicitly.

## Customer market entry hypothesis
Operations/facility managers and field-service software vendors. Do not invent paid pilots or interviews. The Master Book recommends 5 operator interviews and 2 software builder interviews; users decide whether to do them.

## Judges' five criteria
Founder profile, product potential, market viability, technical architecture, meaningful Google AI. No official numeric weighting published in the Master Book; prior 44/50 was subjective, **not** official or achieved. Team resumes/metrics require real evidence.
